Wage threshold for third-country nationals in Hungary

When a non-EU national applies for a work-based residence permit, the authority compares the offered base wage with the national average for the occupation. Enter the FEOR code to see the figure you need to exceed.

    HUF

    Wage thresholdKSH, 2025

    Choose an occupation to see the threshold.

    The rule

    Under Government Decree 445/2013 (XI. 28.), the base wage is considered substantially lower than the national gross average earnings of the occupation if it does not exceed 80% of that average. The average is the figure the Hungarian Central Statistical Office (KSH) publishes for full-time employees by FEOR occupation code.

    • The threshold must be exceeded. A base wage of exactly 80% is not enough.
    • The test applies to the base wage. Bonuses and allowances do not count, although the KSH average includes them.
    • For a work permit, a low wage is a ground on which the authority may refuse. For the single permit, it is part of the labour authority's opinion.
    • Some categories need no permit and are outside the wage test. The EU Blue Card has its own, higher minimum.
    • Since 6 June 2026, new guest-worker residence permits cannot be applied for; permits valid on 5 June 2026 can be renewed.

    Legal basis: Government Decree 445/2013, Section 2 point 13, Section 6 (2) g) and (3), Section 20 (5) d) and (6) (in Hungarian). Checked: 2026-11-20. Data: KSH STADAT 20.1.1.52, 2025 figures, downloaded 2026-11-20. This page is information, not legal advice.
